### 👩🏽‍💻 About me

- 💼 **Software Developer at AXIS Capital** (since 2020: data analyst → intern → developer). I turn Excel-driven pricing and underwriting workflows into **C# / .NET + React** apps on **Azure**, including a central pricing platform replacing **80+ legacy workbooks**.
- 🎓 **B.Sc. Computer Science (Co-op)**, St. Francis Xavier University
- 🧠 If software controls money, risk or decisions, "mostly correct" isn't good enough. I care about **correctness, clear business rules and maintainability**.
- 🌱 After hours I build my own products: Canadian tax tools, booking, vehicle management, and a site for my meal-prep business.
- 🏃🏽‍♀️ Former varsity track & field athlete · Top 64, March Madness Pitch Competition (2021)
